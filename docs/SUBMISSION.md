## 1. Ringkasan 30 detik

**Apa.** Agen otonom di Robinhood Chain sudah saling membayar lewat rel x402/MPP dalam USDG dengan skema `exact`: bayar dulu, terima kemudian, tanpa recourse. AegisClear menaruh **micro-escrow state channel** di alamat `payTo` x402: klien mendanai satu channel USDG per pasangan agen, setiap unit layanan menghasilkan receipt yang ditandatangani kedua pihak (off-chain), dan bila klien menuntut penalti SLA ia mengirim **bukti Groth16** — kontrak memverifikasi (verifier **229.241 gas**, `contracts/test/Verifier.t.sol`; README.id.md §Ringkasan produk) dan membagi dana **proporsional** tanpa pernah melihat harga, ambang, atau metrik. Evaluatornya sirkuit, bukan manusia atau LLM (spec §0).

**Angka yang menjelaskan produk** (spec §0, §6.5, EX1): 100 request @ 0,02 USDG, 7 melanggar SLA latensi → x402 `exact`: 2,00 USDG hilang tanpa recourse; ERC-8183 evaluator: 0 **atau** 2,00 (biner); **AegisClear: 1,93 ke provider / 0,07 kembali ke klien**, deterministik, chain hanya melihat dua komitmen + pembagian akhir.

**Kenapa sekarang.** MeshGateway (dibaca 19 Sep 2026): 1.030 storefront, **15.718 settlement on-chain, 512,48 USDG seumur hidup** — rata-rata 0,033 USDG per settlement (spec §2.3): rel M2M-nya ada, volumenya masih sen karena tanpa recourse pembeli memperkecil ukuran pembelian (§2.2). ERC-8183 (25 Feb 2026) mengakui HTTP 200 tidak cukup, tapi evaluatornya biner & dipercaya penuh (§2.1 P3). USDG di mainnet 4663 bukan token uji: supply **703.768.785 USDG** (§2.3).

**Yang sudah jadi (dibangun 19–20 Sep 2026, 94 commit):** kontrak Solidity (channel EIP-1167, factory CREATE2, verifier Groth16, treasury router), sirkuit circom (**115.066 constraint**, proving **≈ 4,1–4,7 s**; `docs/TOOLCHAIN.md`), SDK TypeScript (provider Hono + klien + watcher), program **Stylus/Rust `AegisPoseidon`** untuk anchored mode, web console, dan **deploy v2 (bytecode teraudit) di Robinhood Chain testnet 46630 — 7/7 kontrak terverifikasi Blockscout, 15 test integrasi lulus on-chain (4 dilewati otomatis, khusus kunci Anvil)** (README.id.md §Status).

**Yang jujur (nyatakan sebelum ditanya):** (1) **trusted setup satu kontributor** — ptau *dan* zkey dibangkitkan lokal oleh penulis karena mirror ptau Hermez HTTP 403 (README.id.md §Trusted setup); kerugian dibatasi kontrak `payToClient ≤ A` (FR-18), tetapi ceremony ≥ 3 kontributor wajib sebelum dana nyata. (2) **MockUSDG di testnet** — USDG asli tidak ada di 46630 (spec V4c). (3) **Mainnet 4663 belum di-deploy** — deployer 0 ETH pada 20 Sep 2026 (spec P1 §C "di luar cakupan"). (4) **Self-audit saja** (Slither 0.11.5 + review manual, 0 true High/Medium, `docs/audit/slither-2026-09.md`), bukan audit independen. (5) Pendanaan lewat **facilitator Mesh nyata belum diuji** (V8 terbuka); di demo klien mentransfer USDG langsung ke alamat `payTo` yang diprediksi (`sdk/src/client/agent.ts`), jalur Permit2/proxy x402 diuji di Foundry (`Permit2.t.sol`).

## 4. Portal write-up (English)

### 4.1 Title, one-liner, track

- **Project name:** AegisClear
- **One-liner (≤ 120 chars):** Privacy-preserving escrow for agent-to-agent USDG payments on Robinhood Chain — SLA disputes settled by a Groth16 proof, not a judge.
- **Prize track:** Overall Prize (also fits "Promising Products Track"); building on **Robinhood Chain** (Arbitrum dedicated chain) — the organizer reserves at minimum 1 of 3 prizes for a Robinhood Chain project and gives extra consideration to USDG integration (HackQuest page, 20 Sep 2026).
- **Tech tags:** Solidity, Rust/Stylus, circom/Groth16 (snarkjs), TypeScript, Foundry, x402, USDG, Permit2, ERC-8183, EIP-712, EIP-1167.

### 4.2 Problem

Agents on Robinhood Chain already pay each other in USDG over x402/MPP rails (MeshGateway: 1,030 storefronts, 15,718 on-chain settlements, 512.48 USDG lifetime — average 0.033 USDG per settlement, read 19 Sep 2026; spec §2.3). The rails use x402 `exact`: pay first, receive later, no recourse — "the Facilitator cannot modify the amount or destination", and cannot hold it either (spec §2.1 P1). That is fine for cents; for machine work worth tens to thousands of dollars the buyer carries 100 % of the non-performance risk, so buyers keep purchases tiny and high-value jobs stay off-chain with invoices and humans (§2.2). ERC-8183 (Agentic Commerce, Feb 2026) adds per-job escrow, but its evaluator is a single trusted party with a binary `complete`/`reject` outcome and public terms; its own text says the evaluator "MAY be a smart contract … verifying a zero-knowledge proof" — nobody had built that (§0). A transparent escrow has a second problem on a public chain: every dispute leaks unit prices, SLA thresholds and telemetry to competitors (§2.1 P7).

### 4.3 Solution

AegisClear is that evaluator, and it is a **circuit**, not a human or an LLM:

1. **Micro-escrow state channel at the x402 `payTo`.** The provider's 402 offer carries `payTo` = a CREATE2-predicted `AegisChannel` clone (EIP-1167) that can only become a channel with the exact terms the provider signed (`AegisChannelFactory.predict/open`, spec §8.5, T19). The client funds it in USDG; each service unit yields a receipt `(seq, qty, m1, m2, due)` and a cumulative checkpoint signed by both parties (EIP-712) — off-chain, zero transactions per unit. The chain only sees `termsCommitment` (Poseidon of price, thresholds, penalty bps, cap bps, nonce) and `receiptsRoot` (Poseidon Merkle root, 128 slots).
2. **ZK settlement.** To claim an SLA penalty, the client proves in Groth16 (BN254, circom, 115,066 constraints; `docs/TOOLCHAIN.md`) that "under the committed terms and the co-signed receipts, the lawful penalty is X" — `payToClient = min(Σ breach_i · ⌊due_i·π/10000⌋, ⌊A·κ/10000⌋)` (spec §6.3). `SLASettlementVerifier` verifies it (**229,241 gas** measured, `contracts/test/Verifier.t.sol`) and the channel splits funds **proportionally**; the contract additionally enforces `payToClient ≤ A` on-chain regardless of the verifier (FR-18). Without a proof, the challenge window pays exactly the last co-signed checkpoint — never more, never less (G5). `settle()` is permissionless.
3. **Payouts to any address** (`payoutClient`/`payoutProvider` set at `open`) plus `AegisTreasuryRouter`: an agent maps itself to a treasury (Safe / ERC-4337 account) and the channel's payout hook forwards funds in the same transaction; a frozen destination becomes a claimable credit instead of a stuck settle (README §How it works).
4. **Epoch rollover** (FR-10, `MAX_SEQ` = 128 units per epoch): pay out the old epoch, keep the remaining deposit as the next epoch's budget, one deposit for many epochs.
5. **Anchored mode** (FR-25) for high-value / low-frequency jobs: every ack is an on-chain `ack(seq, leaf, cumulativeAmount, sigProvider)`; the contract inserts the **leaf hash** (not the raw metrics) into a depth-7 incremental Poseidon Merkle tree — 7 hashes per ack computed by the **Stylus** program `AegisPoseidon`.

Worked example (spec §6.5, EX1, executed on-chain): 100 units at 0.02 USDG, 7 latency breaches, penalty 50 %, cap 30 % → provider 1.93 USDG, client refund 0.07 USDG, unused 3.00 USDG back to the client. Same inputs on an ERC-8183-style binary escrow: 0 or 2.00. What the explorer shows for the AegisClear channel: parties, deposit, `A`, `seq`, `T`, `R`, `payToClient` — never price, thresholds, metrics or the number of breaches (spec §6.7; `leak-check` scans every tx's calldata and logs: **0 leaks**).

### 4.4 What is novel (and what is not)

- **Not new, credited:** escrow between agents (ERC-8183), agent identity (ERC-8004), usage-based payment (x402 `upto`, MPP `session`), Mesh rails, human/optimistic adjudication (Kleros, UMA), ZK on Stylus (Renegade, zk-sunade, ZeroStyl, OpenZeppelin Poseidon2), state channels (Raiden/Perun) — spec §1 "Prior art".
- **New:** (a) the evaluator is a circuit — no person or LLM decides, the result is deterministic, and the evaluator never sees the data; (b) settlement is **proportional** from a committed penalty schedule, not binary; (c) commercial terms and telemetry stay **private** with a published leakage table and an on-chain leak-check, not an "anonymous" claim; (d) it lives at the x402 **`payTo`** address, so facilitators and the canonical `x402ExactPermit2Proxy` (`0x402085c2…20001`) need no change (spec §10.2); (e) one settlement semantics implemented three times (Python reference, TypeScript SDK, circom circuit, plus the contract's payout rule) and tested differentially with shared vectors (`vectors/`, spec §13).

### 4.5 Stylus: where, and the measured why

Stylus is used in exactly one place: on-chain Poseidon hashing for anchored mode (`stylus/aegis-poseidon`, Rust `no_std`, a 1:1 port of circomlib Poseidon **v1** from `circomlibjs/poseidon_opt.js`, so on-chain hashes are identical to the circuit and the SDK). Measured on Robinhood Chain testnet 46630 with `cast estimate` against deployed contracts (`docs/benchmarks/poseidon.md` §5): `insertPath` (7 hashes, the exact call `ack` makes) **144,076 gas Stylus vs 252,271 Yul → 1.75× cheaper (≈ 1.9× execution-only)**; a single `hash2` is **cheaper in Yul** (62,138 vs 82,593) because this chain has no Stylus CacheManager and every call pays program init — so the contract batches the 7 hashes in one call. Live `ack` on testnet v2: 352,421 gas first (cold storage), then 206,615–212,830 in the integration suite, and 349,751 / 201,020–210,087 in the console run (Yul on Anvil: 449,142 / ≈ 303–309k) (README §Stylus: where, and the measured why; §Verified). The Groth16 verifier stays in Solidity on purpose: BN254 pairing is a precompile, and a Stylus verifier costs more (256,334 vs 194,396 gas in public benchmarks, spec §0) — we do not claim Stylus makes ZK cheaper. An earlier 2.33× figure came from OpenZeppelin Poseidon2, which is not circuit-compatible; it was retracted and replaced by the 1.75× number above. Program `AegisPoseidon` `0x1027cf7DC26152012ed9Ef949Aa1432Bf1C7ef34`, 21.7 KB compressed, activated; deployment ran a known-answer check `hash2(1,2)` before wiring the anchored factory (README.id.md §Deploy ke testnet 46630, in Indonesian). Plan B `PoseidonPathYul` (`0x804318aE…3766`) is deployed as an apples-to-apples twin.

### 4.9 Honest limitations & roadmap

- **Trusted setup:** single contributor for both Powers of Tau (public Hermez mirrors returned HTTP 403 on 19 Sep 2026) and phase 2; the beacon is a fixed placeholder. A toxic-waste holder could forge proofs, bounded on-chain by `payToClient ≤ A` (FR-18, tested with an always-true mock verifier). Required before any non-demo mainnet funds: a public phase-1 and a ≥ 3-contributor phase-2 ceremony, or universal PLONK (+50–100 % verify gas, D6). Artifacts are verifiable (`snarkjs zkey verify` against the released r1cs/ptau) but not bit-reproducible (README §Honest limitations).
- **Token:** testnet uses `MockUSDG` (6 decimals, permissionless mint) because USDG does not exist on 46630 (spec V4c). **Mainnet 4663 with real USDG is not deployed** — blocked on bridging ETH to the deployer (0 ETH on 20 Sep 2026). USDG freeze/pause is outside the contract's control (T8): a frozen settle leaves funds in the channel until unfreeze; a frozen treasury becomes router credit.
- **Facilitator:** funding through a real x402 facilitator (Mesh) with `payTo` = predicted channel is untested (V8); the SDK client funds by direct ERC-20 transfer; the Permit2/proxy path is covered by Foundry tests (`Permit2.t.sol`). Plain x402 clients without the SDK must not be pointed at a channel `payTo` (T19).
- **Audit:** self-audit only (Slither + manual, `docs/audit/`). Testnet **v2** (block 122,028,843, 20 Sep 2026) carries the self-audit fixes: the two Slither hardenings, the M-1 `InsufficientGas` guard and `HOOK_GAS` = 300k; v1/v0 remain documented as history in the README.
- **Reference SDK gaps** (documented, none affect fund safety): unauthenticated `Aegis-Client` header (one-unit grief), no per-session mutex, sessions never evicted, provider opens before checking funding (README §Honest limitations).
- **Stylus program lifecycle:** `AegisPoseidon` expires after 365 days without calls (`expiryDays`), keepalive 31 days; the automatic keepalive watcher is P2 — funds are always recoverable via pure-EVM `startClose/claimPenalty/settle/closeCooperative` even if the program expires (README, T15).
- **Metadata leakage is accepted, not hidden:** `A/seq` (average price), `payToClient/A` (penalty share), ack timing; anchored mode additionally reveals `A` per ack (spec §6.7).
- **Economics are small by design** (spec §15): at Mesh's lifetime volume a 25 bps fee is ≈ $0.13; 1,000 channels × $200/month → ≈ $500/month. The value is unlocking job **size**, measured by average settlement size, not fee on cents.
- **Roadmap:** mainnet USDG deploy (D1) → ≥ 3-contributor ceremony → Mesh facilitator integration (V8) → Stylus keepalive watcher (T15) → ERC-8004 reputation signal from `Settled` (FR-28) → attestor/zkTLS for metrics the client cannot observe → proof aggregation beyond 128 receipts.
